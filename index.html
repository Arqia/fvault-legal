FV FVault

A personal academic dashboard.

FVault brings academic deadlines, course announcements, and attendance notices into one private dashboard. It is built for personal use by one student.

What it does

FVault connects to Google Classroom with read-only access to show active courses, published coursework, and announcements. It also has adapters planned for BRONE (Moodle) and SIAM at Universitas Brawijaya.

The dashboard runs on a private, self-hosted server. It is not a public service and it is not offered to other users.

Privacy

FVault only reads the data needed for the dashboard. Google OAuth refresh tokens are stored encrypted at rest on the private server. Classroom data is stored in a local SQLite database and is never sold or shared with advertisers.
